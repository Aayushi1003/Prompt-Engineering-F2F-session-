Game rules

Three rounds, one champion team. The facilitator’s decision is final.

Round 1 · The Juggling Act

9 min · up to 25 pts

  1. Race (3 min): one role per sticky note. 1 point per different role, up to 20. Duplicates count once. No phones.
  2. Sort (3 min): place every note in Keep human, AI co-pilot, or AI drafts, I check.
  3. Pitch (30 sec): one role you’d hand to AI tomorrow, and one your team argued about. Best argument, voted by the room: +5.

Round 2 · Prompt Battle

16 min · up to 45 pts

  1. Choose (1 min): one role from your own “AI drafts” or “co-pilot” column, and a level: Bronze 10, Silver 15, Gold 20.
  2. Build (9 min): the Prompt Lead types; the Fact-Checker and IB Lens must both approve before you submit.
  3. Pitch (60 sec): final prompt, output, minutes saved per week.
  4. Score: level points + rubric 0–20 (CRAFT, Accurate, IB-aligned, Usable Monday; peers and AI judge) + 5 for a real time-saved estimate. Max 45.
  5. Student names or personal data in a prompt = entry void. No team scores itself.

Round 3 · Integrity Lightning

7 min · up to 35 pts

  1. Listen (20 sec): the facilitator reads a scenario; discuss quietly.
  2. Vote on “3, 2, 1”: raise green (fine), amber (fine with care) or red (not acceptable). Late or switched cards score 0.
  3. Justify: 5 points per correct card. Best explanation of the round: +5.

Bonus and fair play

Any time

  1. Eagle Eye +5: catch a real AI error (wrong fact, fake reference, IB mismatch) and show the facilitator. Max +10 per team.
  2. Rotate team roles every round so everyone prompts.
  3. Ties are broken by the higher Round 2 score.
  4. Help other tables when they’re stuck.

Team boards

Quick rules for Rounds 1 and 2

Round 1: 1 point per different role (max 20), sort every role into a column, best-argued contested role +5. Round 2: pick a role from your own board and a level (Bronze 10 · Silver 15 · Gold 20); the Fact-Checker and IB Lens approve before you save; no student names or personal data. All rules
